NUVON LABS Privacy Policy

Nov 4, 2026

NUVON Labs Inc. (the ‘Company’) provides a generative AI-based agent platform and AI solution services, and strives to comply with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act, to protect the freedom and rights of data subjects (Users).
In accordance with Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company has established this Privacy Policy to inform data subjects of the purposes and methods for which it uses the personal information they provide, to explain its personal information processing procedures and standards, and to handle related complaints smoothly.

Scope

This Policy applies to the Company website (https://nuvonlabs.ai) and all services provided by the Company.

Article 1. Purposes of Processing, Items Processed, and Retention and Use Periods

The Company processes Users’ personal information for the following purposes. In accordance with Article 15(1) of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company collects and uses the minimum personal information necessary to provide its services, either with the consent of data subjects or to perform a contract. The Company processes the personal information of children under the age of 14 only for the UNO HOME service. In this case, it obtains the consent of the legal guardian in accordance with Article 22-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act and informs children of matters related to personal information processing in a format and language that are easy to understand. The Company does not process the personal information of children under the age of 14 for any other service.

Consent is obtained in writing or electronically (checkbox, electronic signature), with clear notice at the time of collection and through lawful procedures.

Article 2. Processing of Sensitive Information

  1. The Company processes UNO HOME health measurement information (information about health) and facial recognition information (biometric information used to identify individuals) separately from other personal information and with the separate consent of data subjects, in accordance with Article 23 of the Personal Information Protection Act.
  2. UNO HOME facial images are processed only within the device and are not transmitted to servers. Only measurement results are encrypted and stored on servers.
  3. Possibility of disclosure of sensitive information: UNO HOME health measurement information is displayed on the device screen in the home, so other family members may see it. Members can limit the sharing scope or turn off the display in the device settings.

Article 3. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company processes personal information only within the scope of the purposes of processing and does not provide personal information to third parties without consent. However, the Company provides personal information to third parties only in cases that fall under Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, such as where special provisions exist in law or where it is unavoidable to comply with legal obligations.

Article 4. Entrustment of Personal Information Processing

The Company entrusts personal information processing as follows for the smooth handling of personal information.
When entering into an entrustment contract, the Company specifies in the contract or other documents, in accordance with Article 26 of the Personal Information Protection Act, matters regarding responsibility, including the prohibition of processing personal information for purposes other than the entrusted work, technical and administrative safeguards, restrictions on re-entrustment, management and supervision of the processor, and compensation for damages, and supervises whether the processor handles personal information safely. In addition, in accordance with Article 26(6) of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company restricts processors from re-entrusting personal information processing and requires consent for any re-entrustment.

Article 6. Additional Use and Provision of Personal Information

  1. Information collected through product use In accordance with Article 15(3) or Article 17(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act, the Company discloses cases where additional use or provision of personal information without the data subject’s consent occurs continuously within a scope reasonably related to the purpose of collection, and collects usage data to improve product quality. This usage data does not include sensitive customer information (user queries, search results, document content, or document metadata). Examples of usage data include the number of searches per day, the number of result clicks, clicks by data source (e.g., Drive, Confluence), and search latency. The Company does not additionally use or provide UNO HOME health measurement information or facial recognition information.
  2. Information collected when integrating Workspaces for each User and information included in User input The Company’s applications call Workspace APIs (services that can be integrated with the Company’s applications; the Workspaces integrated may differ by User) for processing, and newly generated data is processed to improve interaction and quality. This data is used to provide the services and perform contracts and is not used for any other purpose. It is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) or ML models. All data stored through Workspaces is securely encrypted, and no one other than the User concerned can view it. Inputs, uploads, and integrated content (documents, images, files, text, voice messages, etc.) provided through product use may contain personal information (name, contact information, address, and other personally identifiable information), and this information is deleted according to procedures when the User withdraws. Users must take care not to include the personal information of data subjects. Under the Terms of Service, Users are responsible for incidents caused by their own negligence.
  3. Use of email within the product The Company’s integrated search product shows only search results that Users have access to. Each User is authenticated via SSO, and the Company maintains all permissions based on email addresses. Email addresses may also be used for Workspace integration. The Company’s applications use email addresses only to apply permissions for documents that Users can view.

Article 7. Installation, Operation, and Refusal of Automatic Personal Information Collection Devices

The Company installs and operates devices that automatically collect personal information using ‘cookies’. Cookies are used to keep users logged in for convenience when using the website. Users may allow or refuse cookies, but refusing them may make it difficult to stay logged in. The Company does not collect behavioral information for personalized advertising.
Cookies are small pieces of information that the server used to operate a website sends to the data subject’s browser. They are stored on the user’s computer or mobile device and are automatically sent from the user’s browser when accessing the website. Users can allow or block cookies at any time through their browser settings.

  • Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data
  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > Allow/block third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings > Advanced tab > Block all cookies and manage website data in the Privacy section

Article 8. Procedures and Methods for Destroying Personal Information

When the retention period expires or the purpose of processing is achieved, the Company destroys the information without delay. Where personal information must be retained under internal policy or other laws, it is stored separately and destroyed immediately once the purpose of processing is achieved.

  • Destruction procedure: Personal information for which a reason for destruction arises is destroyed safely after approval according to internal procedures.
  • Destruction method: Electronic files are deleted in a manner that cannot be recovered, and paper documents are shredded. Facial recognition information stored on UNO HOME devices is deleted when the profile is deleted or the device is reset.

Article 9. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Guardians and How to Exercise Them

  1. Data subjects may at any time request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of their personal information, or withdraw their consent (the ‘exercise of rights’).
  2. Data subjects may exercise their rights by email (Chief Privacy Officer, cwy@nuvonlabs.ai) in accordance with Article 41(1) of the Enforcement Decree of the Personal Information Protection Act, and the Company will take action without delay.
  3. Rights may also be exercised through a representative, such as the data subject’s legal guardian or a person delegated by the data subject. In this case, a power of attorney in the form of Annex Form No. 11 of the Notice on Personal Information Processing Methods (No. 2025-5) must be submitted. Legal guardians of children under the age of 14 (UNO HOME) may exercise the above rights with respect to the child’s personal information.
  4. Requests for access to or suspension of processing of personal information may be restricted or refused under Article 35(4) and Article 37(2) of the Personal Information Protection Act.
  5. Regarding requests for correction or deletion, deletion of personal information cannot be requested where other laws specify that the personal information is subject to collection.
  6. Individual members may withdraw their consent by withdrawing their membership in the relevant service. After withdrawal, they cannot use the service, and re-registration may be temporarily restricted.
  7. The Company verifies whether the person exercising the rights of a data subject is the data subject or a legitimate representative.
  8. Data subjects may exercise their rights through the department listed in Article 12, and the Company will handle requests within 10 days of receipt.
  9. Requests to exercise rights regarding personal information the Company processes as a processor (Article 14) must be made to the client that is the controller. If the Company receives such a request, it forwards it to the client without delay.

Article 10. Automated Decisions

The Company does not make decisions using fully automated systems that significantly affect the rights or obligations of data subjects (Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act). UNO HOME health measurements and AI answers are generated automatically but are for reference only and do not determine service eligibility, fees, or similar matters.

Article 11. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

The Company implements technical, administrative, and physical safeguards for the security of personal information and manages personal information securely through access control for personal information processing systems, encryption of personal information, and installation of security programs.

  • Administrative measures: establishing and implementing an internal management plan, providing privacy training, and operating a dedicated organization.
  • Technical measures
    • Access control: access rights to personal information processing systems are minimized, and personal information, including passwords, is encrypted and stored in accordance with legal requirements.
    • Encryption: personal information is transmitted and stored in a securely encrypted state.
    • Security programs: security programs are installed to prevent external intrusion and protect personal information.
  • Physical measures: access control and access logs for key protected areas such as server rooms, control of the removal and import of auxiliary storage media, and storage of important materials in secure places with locks.
