About Beauty AI

Skin analysis that starts with a smartphone photo

Beauty AI distinguishes front and left/right side images, aligns face position and angle, then analyzes skin areas and the condition of each item. Results are delivered as a report showing scores, conditions and analyzed locations for each skin item. Development currently focuses on mobile use by general users, with future expansion to beauty brands, stores and professional consultation settings.
