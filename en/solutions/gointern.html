About GoIntern

Your team works with AI, and admins shape the work environment

GoIntern consists of a user service where team members use AI in their daily work, and an admin service for managing the organization’s AI agents, users, content, and integrations. By providing both the screens for using AI and the admin environment for running it to fit your organization, it helps companies put AI to work in real business.
