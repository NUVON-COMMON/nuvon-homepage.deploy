The Challenge

Why attendance management gets complicated

When check-ins, work schedules, and approvals are managed in different ways, you end up hunting for missing records and rechecking work hours at the end of every month.

  1. 01

    Missing records

    Missed check-ins and late entries from field, off-site, and shift work

  2. 02

    Complex work rules

    Different hour calculations and exceptions for each work type

  3. 03

    Scattered history

    Leave, off-site, and attendance approvals left in chats or verbal notes

  4. 04

    Repetitive month-end work

    Repeated work from gathering check-in records to verifying hours

Working environments are changing, records should be clearer

As work types and employment relationships diversify, records of who worked when and under which rules matter more. time·lit manages attendance data—from check-ins to work hours and approval history—under consistent rules.

How It Works

Just record it—it is organized by your work rules.

Employee check-ins and approval results are applied to your work rules to calculate work hours. Managers can review work hours and exceptions right away.

  1. 01

    Check in and out

    Employees record check-ins on mobile based on location and time.

  2. 02

    Apply work rules

    Calculation rules set by work type and organization are applied.

  3. 03

    Calculate and review

    Review work hours and exceptions, including approval results.
