The Challenge

Why Attendance Management Gets Complicated

When attendance records, work schedules, and approval histories are managed separately, teams spend every month-end tracking missing records and recalculating work hours.

  1. 01

    Missing Records

    Missed clock-ins and late entries in field, off-site, and shift work

  2. 02

    Complex Work Rules

    Different time calculations and exception handling for each work type

  3. 03

    Scattered Records

    Leave, off-site, and attendance approvals left in messengers or given verbally

  4. 04

    Repetitive Month-End Tasks

    Recurring work from consolidating attendance records to verifying work hours

How It Works

Every Record Is Organized Automatically Based on Work Rules

Clock-in/out records and approval results are processed according to configured work rules to calculate work hours. Managers can quickly review work hours and exceptions.

  3. 03
