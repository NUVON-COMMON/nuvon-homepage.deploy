About Us

Home healthcare that starts at the mirror

UNO Home is a home healthcare solution that combines contactless health measurement, face authentication, family health management, and radar-based sleep and fall monitoring in an Android-based mirror display. Simply standing in front of the mirror lets you check health indicators and keep track of changes in your sleep and daily life, with no wearable required.

Core Technology

Measures your health and keeps personal data on the device.

UNO Home collects health and sleep data using camera-based contactless measurement, face recognition, and 60GHz radar. Sensitive raw data such as faces and video is processed on the device, and only the necessary results are sent to the server.

  • RPPG

    Contactless health measurement

    Analyzes subtle changes in skin color from camera video to measure health metrics such as heart rate and HRV.

  • Vision AI

    Face recognition and personalization

    Recognizes faces to tell users apart and links each one to their registered profile and health data.

  • 60GHz Radar

    Sleep and fall detection

    Detects presence, posture, and falls with 60GHz radar, and records movement and breathing changes during sleep.

  • On-Device AI

    Privacy protection

    Raw data such as faces and video is processed on the device, and only the necessary results are sent to the server.
