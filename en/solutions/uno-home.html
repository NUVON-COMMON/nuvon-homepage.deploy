    • Core Technology

    Measure Health. Keep Personal Data on Device.

    UNO HOME uses camera-based contactless measurement, facial recognition, and 60 GHz radar to collect health and sleep information. Sensitive raw data, including facial images and video, is processed on the device, while only necessary results are sent to the server.

    • rPPG

      Contactless Health Checks

      Subtle skin color changes in camera video are analyzed to measure heart rate, HRV, and other health indicators.

    • Vision AI

      Face ID & Personalization

      Faces are recognized to identify users and link them to their registered profiles and health information.

    • 60 GHz Radar

      Sleep & Fall Detection

      60 GHz radar detects presence, posture, and falls, and records movement and breathing changes during sleep.

    • On-Device AI

      Privacy Protection

      Raw data such as facial images and video is processed on the device, and only the necessary results are sent to the server.
